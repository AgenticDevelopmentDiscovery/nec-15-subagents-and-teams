# Conclusion — spine

## Purpose

Close the loop — restate what the reader can now do (must match the promise in 01-context exactly), point at specific next steps in a sensible order, and name what is still genuinely open so the tutorial ends honestly.

## Claims

- The five capabilities promised in 01-context are delivered — this section restates them as a check
- The pattern taught here is the unit of composition for every agentic framework in this course — this earns the "why it belongs" framing from topic.md
- The make-or-break decision heuristic is the best available, not a solved problem — said plainly
- Coordination overhead is hard to predict even with experience — honest about the limits
- Agent team features are experimental and moving — the audience should not treat the mechanics as permanent

## Decisions

- Listed capabilities as bullets matching 01-context exactly — the panel checks these against each other; keeping them identical closes that finding before it opens
- Ordered "where to go next" by specificity — Claude Code docs first (what they just used), then CrewAI and LangGraph (structured frameworks), then AutoGen and cross-session (conceptual extension) — a reader can stop at any point and have a useful next step
- Kept open edges honest and specific — three real unsettled things, not vague "the field is evolving" filler

## Open questions

- The closing line of "What you can do now" connects back to the course — is that the right register for a conclusion slide, or does it read as overselling?

## Not doing

- A summary of everything covered — the capability list already does this job without repeating prose
- Recommending one framework over others — out of scope and likely to age badly
- Covering agent memory, security, or consensus patterns — explicitly out of scope per topic.md; not named here so reviewers do not flag them as gaps
