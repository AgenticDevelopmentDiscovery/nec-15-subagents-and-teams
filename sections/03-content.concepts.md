# Content — spine

## Purpose

Let the audience choose an approach, reveal the answer, then explain implementation.

## Claims

- A short test-failure report is a bounded subagent task
- Competing hypotheses and changing cross-module contracts can benefit from team discussion
- Independent module research and independent review reports can use parallel subagents
- Several perspectives alone do not require an agent team
- Reusable agent definitions separate instructions from individual invocations
- Team startup requires the experimental setting and an interactive session
- Context separation does not imply separate files or permissions

## Decisions

- Every example has a neutral question slide followed immediately by its answer and approach
- Use the mixed answer order: subagent, team, team, subagents, subagents
- Keep source links on answer slides to avoid revealing answers on question slides
- State recommendations for the specific constraints, not universal requirements
- Put the reusable agent definition and team launch after all examples
- Preserve the chair-design demo handoff after setup
- Keep the original slide theme and existing illustrations

## Open questions

- The separate presenter supplies the chair demonstration and its results

## Not doing

- Running example tasks or presenting their hypothetical outputs as measurements
- A live framework implementation or a new critique round
